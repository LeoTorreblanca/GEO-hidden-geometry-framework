# GEO Effective Law

## Active fraction

The active geometric fraction is represented by:

fc

During the exploratory tests, stable values appeared near:

fc ≈ 0.74 → 0.774

with a preferred region near:

sqrt(3/5)

---

## Complementary sector

The complementary fraction is:

fout = 1 - fc

This is interpreted as the non-active or partially decoupled complementary response.

---

## Effective coupling

The effective response parameter can be written schematically as:

mu_eff

The final explored response law was:

R = mu_eff^(1/3)

where:

R

represents the effective growth response ratio.

---

## Cosmological interpretation

GEO proposes that the observable sector does not necessarily receive the full structural growth response.

Instead, the observed growth can be interpreted as geometrically filtered or damped.
