GEO — Hidden Geometry Framework

Leonel Hernán Torreblanca

GEO (Geometría Oculta / Hidden Geometry Framework) is an exploratory computational cosmology framework investigating whether part of the observed cosmological suppression and transfer behavior may emerge from hidden geometric structures.

The project combines:

  • computational cosmology,
  • CLASS perturbation analysis,
  • growth suppression studies,
  • Hubble tension exploration,
  • S8/fs8 observational behavior,
  • and geometric transfer structures.

The framework is currently presented as:

  • an exploratory geometric framework,
  • a reproducible computational research project,
  • and an open technical investigation subject to validation, criticism and falsification.
