# History of GEO  
## From GDD to Hidden Geometry

**Author:** Leonel Hernan Torreblanca

---

# Introduction

GEO (Geometría Oculta / Hidden Geometry Framework) did not emerge as a predefined cosmological theory.

The framework evolved progressively through multiple exploratory stages focused on:

- cosmological growth suppression,
- geometric consistency,
- transfer structures,
- and observational stability.

This document summarizes the conceptual evolution of the framework.

---

# Stage 1 — GDD  
## Geometría Dual Dinámica

The first stage explored the possibility that gravitational structure and mass behavior could be interpreted through dual geometric relations.

The original investigations focused on:

- invariants,
- internal/external limits,
- effective geometric regions,
- and complex structural interpretations.

At this stage:

- no final transfer law existed,
- no geometric node structure had emerged yet,
- and the work was mostly exploratory.

However, repeated patterns started appearing near stable limit regions.

These limits later became fundamental for GEO.

---

# Stage 2 — SOP  
## Suppression / Sector Exploration

During SOP, the focus shifted toward:

- effective growth suppression,
- transfer fractions,
- and complementary geometric behavior.

This stage introduced:

\[
f_c
\]

as an effective active transfer fraction.

The complementary relation:

\[
f_{out} = 1 - f_c
\]

also emerged naturally.

The key observation was that suppression no longer behaved randomly.

Instead, it started appearing inside constrained regions.

This was the conceptual birth of geometric transfer interpretation.

---

# Stage 3 — LEO  
## Geometric Architectural Structure

LEO represented the transition from numerical coincidence to architectural interpretation.

At this stage:

- geometric nodes began appearing repeatedly,
- preferred regions emerged,
- and stable transfer bands were identified.

Particular attention was given to values near:

\[
\frac34
\]

\[
\sqrt{\frac35}
\]

\[
\frac{\pi}{4}
\]

The framework began behaving less like a phenomenological correction and more like a constrained geometric architecture.

The relation:

\[
\eta = f_c^2
\]

also emerged during this stage.

---

# Stage 4 — GEO  
## Hidden Geometry Framework

GEO represents the consolidation of the previous stages into a single exploratory geometric framework.

The central idea became:

> observable cosmological suppression may emerge from an underlying hidden geometric transfer structure.

The framework investigates whether:

- cosmological growth,
- effective suppression,
- and transfer behavior

can be interpreted geometrically.

At this stage, the predictive relation:

\[
R = \mu^{1/3}
\]

emerged as one of the central effective laws.

---

# Current Status

GEO currently represents:

- an exploratory framework,
- a reproducible numerical investigation,
- and an open geometric interpretation of cosmological growth suppression.

The project has not yet been peer reviewed.

The repository is intended for:

- transparency,
- reproducibility,
- open scientific discussion,
- and independent verification.

---

# Final Note

GEO should not currently be interpreted as a confirmed replacement for standard cosmology.

Instead, it should be viewed as:

- an exploratory geometric framework,
- a mathematical/phenomenological investigation,
- and a proposal for hidden structural organization in cosmological growth behavior.
